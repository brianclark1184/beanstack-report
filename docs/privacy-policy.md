# Privacy Policy — Reading Log for Beanstack

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Reading Log for Beanstack is an independent browser extension that turns a reader's Beanstack calendar into a printable monthly PDF. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Beanstack.

## Information handled

When you invoke the extension on a Beanstack reading calendar, it reads the displayed reader name, reader ID, Beanstack site address, dates, book titles, recorded minutes, and entry statuses such as Completed. You can also enter student, teacher, class, and reading-goal information. The extension uses this information only to create your reading logs and remember your profiles.

## Local storage and retention

Saved student/teacher/class profiles, goals, and linked reader names, IDs, and site addresses are stored in Chrome's local extension storage. The extension does not use Chrome Sync for this data. Reading entries and PDF previews remain in the open editor tab's memory and are released when the tab closes. PDFs you download remain in your chosen download location until you delete them. Local storage and downloaded PDFs are not separately encrypted by this extension; use your computer's normal account and device protections.

You can edit or delete saved profiles in the extension. Uninstalling the extension removes its local settings. It does not delete PDFs you previously downloaded or reading history in Beanstack. Installing a separate Web Store copy does not automatically transfer settings from an unpacked development copy.

## Sharing and network activity

The extension has no developer-operated backend and does not send student information, reading records, or saved profiles to the developer, OpenAI, advertisers, or analytics services. It uses no AI service, tracking, or advertising. PDF code and fonts are bundled with the extension.

The extension uses your existing Beanstack session and Beanstack's own previous/next month controls. Those normal page requests go to your Beanstack site over HTTPS and remain subject to Beanstack's privacy practices. The extension does not read your passwords or authentication cookies. Chrome Web Store installation and updates are handled by Google under Google's policies. Opening external support or privacy links is normal browsing to the linked service.

## Permissions

- **activeTab:** Temporary access to the tab where you click the extension icon. The extension accepts supported HTTPS Beanstack reader-calendar pages.
- **scripting:** Run bundled extraction code on that calendar and operate its month-navigation controls. The extension does not add, edit, or delete Beanstack reading records.
- **storage:** Save your profiles and preferences locally.

It does not request access to all websites or Chrome's browsing-history database. It reads the current Beanstack address to identify the site and reader.

## Limited Use

The extension's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Information is used only for the extension's reading-log features. User data is not sold, used for advertising, transferred for unrelated purposes, or used to determine creditworthiness or lending eligibility. The developer has no remote access to your locally stored information.

## Support and changes

For questions, use the project's issue tracker: https://github.com/brianclark1184/beanstack-report/issues. Do not post student names, credentials, or private reading records in public issues. If you voluntarily share information in a support request, it is handled by the service you use to submit that request.

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date. Unlisted distribution means anyone with the Chrome Web Store listing link can install the extension; it is not an access-control mechanism.
