Privacy & permissions

Everything is processed on your computer

This independent private beta does not use AI, analytics, advertising, or a backend server. It does not send student information or reading records to its developer or OpenAI.

Student, teacher, class, goals, and the linked Beanstack reader name, ID, and site are saved in Chrome’s local extension storage. They are not synced by this extension. Delete a profile to remove it, or uninstall the extension to remove all its stored settings.

Reading entries are held in the open extension tab’s memory. Closing that tab releases them. Downloaded PDFs remain in your download folder until you delete them.

Permissions

activeTab and scripting: Read the Beanstack calendar you invoke the extension on and use its previous/next month buttons. It uses your existing login and does not read passwords or change reading entries. Month navigation communicates with Beanstack as normal browsing does.

storage: Save your profiles and preferences locally. No access to browsing history, cookies, or all websites is requested.

Private distribution limits who receives the package; it does not encrypt downloaded PDFs or prevent recipients from sharing the package. This tool is not affiliated with or endorsed by Beanstack.
